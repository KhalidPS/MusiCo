MusiCo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.k.sekiro.musico

The short version

MusiCo is an offline music player. Your music, playlists and listening history stay on your phone — they are never uploaded to us or anyone else, and there is no account to create.

The app does send anonymous usage and crash statistics to Google Firebase, which helps fix bugs. That is the only data that leaves your device automatically, and it is described in full below.

Who is responsible

MusiCo is an independent app developed by an individual developer. There is no company behind it, no analytics broker, no advertising network and no data is sold or shared for marketing. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the contact address at the bottom.

What stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone. It is stored in a private app database that only MusiCo can read, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Your music files. MusiCo reads the audio already on your device to play it. It never uploads, copies or transmits your audio to any server.
  • Music details read from your files — title, artist, album, duration, date added and file location — used to build the library, search and the artist/album views.
  • Your playlists, favourites and recently played list.
  • Playback state, such as the current song and position.

What is sent off your device

MusiCo uses two Google Firebase services. Neither receives your music, your file names, your playlists or your listening history.

Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting

When the app crashes, a report is sent so the fault can be found and fixed. It contains the technical state of the crash: the error and stack trace, the device model, the Android version, the app version, and how long the app had been running.

Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics

Aggregate, non-identifying information about how the app is used — for example how often it is opened, and on which device models and Android versions — so effort goes to the things people actually use. It includes a randomly generated app instance identifier, your approximate region (derived from your IP address by Google, not stored by us), device model, and language.

Advertising identifier

Firebase Analytics can access your device's Android Advertising ID. MusiCo shows no adverts and does not use this identifier to profile or target you, but it is technically available to the analytics library, so it is disclosed here rather than left unsaid.

You can reset or delete this identifier at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device, which also lets you opt out of personalisation entirely.

Data handled by these services is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. See also Firebase and privacy.

Sharing playlists and sending songs

MusiCo can share a playlist as a QR code, and can send the actual audio files to another phone running MusiCo. Both work without any server of ours:

  • A shared QR code contains only the playlist's own details — its name and the songs in it. It is displayed on your screen; it is not uploaded anywhere.
  • A song transfer runs over a direct Wi-Fi link between the two phones, using a temporary local address on that link. The files go straight from one device to the other. They do not pass through the internet, and we never receive or store them.
  • Each transfer session uses a one-time token, so another device on the network cannot join a transfer it was not invited to.
  • Nothing is sent until the receiving person accepts, and either side can stop at any point.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling MusiCo deletes its database — playlists, favourites and history. Your actual music files are yours and are left untouched. You can also clear everything without uninstalling, in Settings → Apps → MusiCo → Storage → Clear data.
  • Crash and analytics data: held by Google under their retention schedules. To stop analytics, uninstall the app; to reset the identifier tied to it, use the advertising settings noted above.

Children

MusiCo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It has no accounts, no profiles, no messaging and no user-generated public content.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it collects or which permissions it needs, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
